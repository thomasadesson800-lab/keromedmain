Shipping & Returns Info


Shipping Policy

We deliver across all Egyptian governorates. Shipping fee for Minya and New Minya is 30 EGP, while other governorates range between 100 EGP to 250 EGP. Pay with Visa to get 30% off shipping!

Returns & Exchange

Unopened products can be returned within 14 days of receipt. Please ensure hygienic seals remain intact for medical safety.

Frequently Asked Questions


Q: How do I pay for my order?

A: You can pay via Cash on Delivery or using your Visa / Credit Card. Visa payments receive 30% off shipping costs!

Q: What discounts apply to medical scrubs and apparel?

A: When purchasing wearable items, buy 2 apparels get 10% off, buy three or more get 20% off.

Q: What if I am not available to answer the phone?

A: We collect a mandatory alternative phone number to ensure your order arrives on time.
